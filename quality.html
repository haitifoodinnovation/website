Our evidence commitment. Haiti Food Innovation ET is committed to documented quality, hygiene, traceability and food safety. Our internal laboratory supports product development, process observation and quality trending; for independent analytical evidence we work with accredited Bless Agri Food Laboratory Services PLC and retain product- and sample-specific test reports.
Impact & sourcing

Designed to create value beyond the factory gate

  • Employment: more than 136 full-time employees.
  • Distribution: eleven distribution centres serving established markets.
  • Women & rural suppliers: partnerships with small, women-dominated rural enterprises producing kocho around Bonga.
  • Supplier development: training and contracting of additional small enterprises for kocho and bulla supply, with quality, hygiene and sourcing documentation.
  • Food-system contribution: greater local value addition through everyday staples, packaged ingredients and responsibly developed new formats.

HAITIFood Innovation · ET
